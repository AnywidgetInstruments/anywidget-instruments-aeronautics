# Standards and references

The library follows conventions described in the documents below. It claims no
conformity with any of them: it is neither certified nor assessed against them, and a
figure from one of them is quoted in this documentation only after checking it against
the official text.

| Document | What the library takes from it |
|---|---|
| EASA CS-23 and CS-25, FAA 14 CFR Parts 23 and 25 | The arrangement of the flight instruments (basic T) and the markings of the airspeed indicator |
| FAA Advisory Circular 25-11 | Conventions of electronic flight displays: layout of a primary flight display, colours of indications |
| SAE ARP 4102-7 | Symbology of electronic primary flight displays |
| ICAO Annex 5 | Units of measurement used in air and ground operations |

The widgets are original drawings modelled on the meaning of these conventions, not
reproductions of any aircraft's instruments.
